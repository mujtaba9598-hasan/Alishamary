Built for the Trade

We manufacture commercial-grade fishing gear that stands up to the realities of the ocean. From our facility in Malin, County Donegal, we build robust parlour pots and creels designed specifically for the lobster and crab fishing industries.

We understand that your gear is your livelihood. That’s why we use heavy-duty steel frames, premium netting, and protective bumper rubbering.

ABOUT OUR MANUFACTURING

Uncompromising Quality

Every pot and creel is hand-assembled in Ireland using materials proven to withstand the harshest ocean environments.

Welded Steel Frames

Our frames are precision-welded from industrial-grade steel to prevent deformation under extreme weight and pressure.

Custom Braided Netting

We use tightly woven, UV-resistant netting designed to trap crabs securely without tearing on jagged rocks.

Impact Bumpers

Heavy rubbering is applied to high-impact zones to protect the pots when hauling aboard the vessel.
