1. Introduction

Alisha Mary Fisheries Ltd ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 of Ireland. It explains how we collect, use, and protect any information you provide when using our website.

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data:

  • Identity Data: Name, business name, and job title.
  • Contact Data: Billing address, delivery address, email address, and telephone numbers.
  • Financial Data: Payment details (processed securely via encrypted third-party gateways).
  • Transaction Data: Details about payments to and from you and details of products you have purchased.

3. How We Use Your Data

Under GDPR, we only process your personal data where we have a lawful basis to do so. We use your data for:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing wholesale and retail orders, manufacturing gear to spec, and arranging delivery.
  • Legitimate Interests: Managing customer service inquiries, waitlists, and providing updates on product availability.
  • Legal Obligations: Retaining financial records for compliance with the Revenue Commissioners in Ireland.

4. Data Security & Storage

We have implemented appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Data is stored on secure servers located within the European Economic Area (EEA).

5. Your Legal Rights

Under Irish and EU data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Request the transfer of your personal data to another party.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@alishamaryfisheries.com.

6. Contacting the Regulator

If you have a concern about our data processing, you have the right to make a complaint at any time to the Data Protection Commission (DPC), the Irish supervisory authority for data protection issues (www.dataprotection.ie).
